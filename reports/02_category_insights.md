# InsightPilot - Category Performance

## Dataset

Synthetic retail transactions from January 2025 through October 2026.

## Key Findings

- Highest revenue: Home
- Highest profit: Home
- Highest profit margin: Beauty (50.99%)
- Lowest profit margin: Home (46.25%)
- Total revenue: CAD $1,661,105.99
- Total profit: CAD $792,654.99

## Interpretation

Revenue leadership does not necessarily mean margin leadership. Categories with lower margins may require further review of pricing, costs, and discounts.
