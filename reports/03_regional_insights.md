# InsightPilot - Regional Sales Analysis

## Dataset

Synthetic Canadian retail transactions from January 2025 through October 2026.

## Key Findings

- Highest revenue region: Alberta
- Highest profit region: Alberta
- Highest margin: Quebec (48.02%)
- Lowest margin: Ontario (47.28%)
- Top regional revenue share: 20.50%
- Total revenue: CAD $1,661,105.99
- Total profit: CAD $792,654.99

## Business Interpretation

Regions with high revenue do not necessarily have the highest profit margins.

Investigate regional pricing, product mix, promotions, and operating costs before making business decisions.

Customer counts are distinct within each region and may overlap across regions.

**Important:** All data is synthetic. Regional differences are simulated and do not represent actual market performance.
